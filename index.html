About

Research-oriented lecturer in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence with experience in healthcare AI (medical imaging), NLP and text mining, and robust model evaluation. I hold an MS in Data Science, with thesis research in time-series forecasting, focused on reproducible experiments, feature engineering and generalisation under non-stationarity.

I teach core AI and ML courses, supervise applied ML projects, and build ML pipelines from experiment to deployment. I am open to PhD research topics in deep learning, evaluation and generalisation, including healthcare AI, computer vision, NLP, trustworthy ML and time-series learning.
